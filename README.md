# Sunday & Ink Launchpad

Create the new production-quality main website for SUNDAY & INK, a premium web design, branding, and digital growth studio. This replaces the old Stay Story positioning and must be broad enough to serve vacation-rental owners AND general small businesses such as contractors, restaurants, salons, photographers, professional services, boutique hospitality, and other owner-led businesses.

THIS MUST NOT LOOK LIKE A GENERIC WEB-DESIGN AGENCY TEMPLATE.

PRIMARY BUSINESS GOAL
Convert visitors into qualified inquiries and paying website/branding clients. Every major visual effect, section, CTA, and interaction must either create desire, prove capability, increase trust, clarify the offer, or reduce friction. The experience itself should function as proof of Sunday & Ink’s creative ability. Desired reaction: “Whoa — I’ve never seen a small web studio site do this.”

BRAND IDENTITY — PRESERVE EXACTLY
Brand name: SUNDAY & INK (NEVER “Sundays & Ink”).
Tagline: “WEBSITES. BRANDS. BEAUTIFULLY BUILT.”
Visual identity: deep ink-black background; warm ivory/white; metallic brushed gold; dramatic oversized editorial serif typography; an expressive gold ink/brush ampersand; restrained gold ink splatter and brush details. Elegant, artistic, editorial, premium, bold. Do NOT substitute generic black-and-gold luxury, cheesy gradients, corporate navy, beige minimalism, or generic startup styling.

Typography: use a high-contrast editorial display serif for major headlines (Didot/Bodoni/Canela-style feeling, using a legally available web font), paired with a clean modern sans-serif for body/UI. Typography should become part of the visual composition: oversized words, masked reveals, cropped letterforms, controlled overlaps, and dramatic scale changes.

CORE POSITIONING
Sunday & Ink creates custom, conversion-focused websites and brands for businesses that have outgrown generic templates, DIY websites, weak online presence, or marketplace-only identity. Vacation rentals remain a specialty, but DO NOT position the whole studio as Airbnb-only.

Messaging must emphasize:
- beautiful is not enough; the site must convert
- custom design around the individual brand/business
- mobile-first experience
- strategic structure and user journey
- branding and digital presence, not merely “pages”
- booking/payment/lead integrations where relevant
- SEO foundations and analytics
- businesses should look as good online as the work/product/property they actually deliver

DO NOT make unsupported promises such as guaranteed rankings, guaranteed revenue, guaranteed bookings, or specific conversion lifts.

SITE ARCHITECTURE / EXPERIENCE
Do not use a predictable hero → services cards → testimonials → pricing → contact template without major creative reinterpretation. Build a narrative with distinct interaction styles.

1. OPENING / PRELOADER
Create a very short elegant brand reveal: black screen, thin gold ink line draws across, the ampersand feels painted into existence, then SUNDAY & INK resolves sharply. Keep fast enough not to hurt conversion. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

2. HERO — MAKE THIS UNFORGETTABLE
Full viewport. Massive editorial headline broken over the composition, something in the spirit of:
“YOUR BUSINESS
SHOULDN’T LOOK
LIKE EVERYONE ELSE’S.”
Supporting copy: “We design high-converting websites and brands that make the right people stop, trust, click and buy.”
Primary CTA: “START YOUR PROJECT”
Secondary CTA: “SEE OUR WORK”
Small proof line: “Custom websites • Brand identity • Conversion strategy”

Use a reactive gold ink treatment in the hero: pointer movement subtly pulls a liquid-gold/ink trail or distortion around the giant ampersand / headline region. This should feel refined, not like a game. On touch devices use an ambient autonomous version.

3. “BEAUTIFUL ISN’T THE GOAL.” CONVERSION MANIFESTO
Create an editorial scroll sequence where individual statements occupy the screen and transition with masks/kinetic typography:
“Beautiful gets attention.”
“Clear earns trust.”
“Strategy creates action.”
“We build for all three.”
Then concise explanation that every Sunday & Ink project is structured around the visitor journey and the action the business actually needs: inquiry, call, booking, purchase, reservation, application, etc.

4. BEFORE / AFTER INTERACTIVE TRANSFORMATION
Build a highly visual interactive section showing a generic weak business presence transforming into a Sunday & Ink-level presence. Do NOT use copyrighted or identifiable third-party websites. Create fictional/demo UI only.
Use a draggable reveal or scroll morph where flat/template-like UI transforms into immersive custom design. Label the contrast conceptually: “Just online” → “Impossible to ignore.”
This is key proof of capability.

5. SERVICES — NOT BORING CARDS
Create three major service worlds with different motion/visual treatment:
A) WEB DESIGN — custom websites built around conversion
B) BRAND IDENTITY — visual systems people remember
C) DIGITAL GROWTH — SEO foundations, social assets/management, ongoing optimization and support
Include Vacation Rentals as a clearly visible specialty / sub-path, not the entire identity.
Each service should have a concise outcome-focused description and CTA.

6. INDUSTRIES / WHO WE BUILD FOR
Create an animated typographic or orbiting roster, not a standard grid:
Vacation rentals • Hospitality • Contractors • Restaurants • Salons & beauty • Photographers • Professional services • Local businesses • Founder-led brands
Copy: “Different businesses. Different audiences. Never the same website twice.”

Create a distinctive hover/scroll effect: project screens tilt or unfold in perspective, with subtle cursor-responsive depth.

8. PROOF THROUGH EXPERIENCE
Create a section explicitly connecting this website’s effects to client work, e.g. “If your website feels forgettable, your brand becomes forgettable.” Demonstrate premium micro-interactions live: magnetic CTA, masked typography, perspective card, cursor-reactive gold line, image reveal, smooth transition. Keep effects purposeful and performant.

WOW EFFECTS / INTERACTIONS — MUST FEEL ORIGINAL
Implement a curated set, not everything everywhere:
- metallic-gold fluid/ink cursor trail localized to hero or key transition
- magnetic CTA buttons with subtle spring physics
- scroll-driven oversized type masks
- section transitions where black ink appears to spread/reveal next content
- perspective portfolio tiles responding to pointer
- elegant page-scroll progress rendered like a thin gold brush line
- selected text where gold highlight appears to be physically brushed across words
- one horizontal scroll moment on desktop with a strong vertical/mobile fallback
- subtle ambient grain and ink texture
- occasional gold splatter particles that react to section entrance, not constant confetti
- custom hover states that “draw” borders/underlines rather than simple color swaps
- smooth anchor transitions
- reduced-motion accessibility mode

IMPORTANT: do not sacrifice usability, mobile performance, or conversion for effects. No autoplay sound. No excessive cursor replacement that makes buttons hard to click. No animation should block a CTA.

MOBILE
The mobile version must feel designed, not collapsed desktop. Recompose oversized typography, convert horizontal interactions to swipe/stack, maintain thumb-friendly CTA placement, ensure lead form is effortless, and keep motion lightweight.

SEO / TECHNICAL
Set title/metadata around Sunday & Ink web design, branding, conversion-focused websites, small business websites, and vacation-rental website specialty without keyword stuffing. Include semantic headings, accessible interactions, alt placeholders, strong performance, lazy-load noncritical imagery, and proper focus states.

DO NOT use fake testimonials, fake client counts, fake awards, or fabricated performance stats.

Build the complete polished site now, not a wireframe. The website itself must be strong enough to serve as Sunday & Ink’s best portfolio piece.
